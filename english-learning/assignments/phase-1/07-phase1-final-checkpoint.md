## A — Reading

### Text

Last month I visited my parents in another city. I usually work remotely, but that week I took three days off. On Tuesday morning I got up early because I needed to catch the train. The weather was bad, and I was a bit nervous. On the train I read a book and listened to music. I didn’t have a lot of baggage — only one backpack.

Now I am back in my apartment. I want to visit them again in October. I need to improve my English, especially for travel. At the moment I’m not working on a new feature — I’m resting. English is important for me. I don’t have much free time, but I try to practice every day.

### A1 — True / False (+ коротко почему)

1. He visited his parents last month.  
2. He usually works in the office every day.  
3. He needed to catch the train on Tuesday.  
4. Right now he is developing a new feature.  
5. He wants to visit his parents in October.  
6. He has a lot of free time.

### A2 — Ответь по тексту (EN)

7. Why did he get up early on Tuesday?  
8. What is he doing at the moment?  
9. What does he want to do in October?  
10. Find in the text: 1× Present Simple, 1× Present Continuous, 1× Past Simple, 1× *want to* or *need to*.

Я frontend-разработчик. Обычно я работаю удалённо. Сейчас я сижу дома в своей квартире и пью чай. Вчера я ездил на вокзал. Мне нужно было купить билет. Погода была хорошая, поэтому я не взял зонт.
