## Task

## Mentor feedback

**Вердикт:** хорошо — заметно лучше assessment. Present Simple держится: *I work / I am / I develop / At work / My stack is / He works / They are*. Старых ловушек (*I working*, *On work*, *My stack it is*) почти нет. Оценка за задание: **7/10**. Да, you did well.

### Corrected version

```text
I work five days a week. I am a software developer. I develop web applications. My stack is Vue 3, TypeScript, Pinia, WebRTC, and SIP. At work I develop an app for traders. But I feel I need to improve my skills. My colleagues have a lot of experience. They are really professional. They work a lot. My colleague Sergey has ten years of experience. He is a team lead and a good guy.
```

### Главные 3 пункта

1. ***for a traders*** → ***for traders*** / ***an app for traders*** — *a* только перед единственным числом (*a trader*), не перед *traders*.
2. ***I But feel… up my competition*** → ***But I feel I need to improve my skills*** — *But* в начале; *competition* ≠ «конкурентоспособность/скиллы»; chunk: *need to improve…*
3. ***They work at many time*** / ***works ten years*** → ***They work a lot*** · ***Sergey has ten years of experience*** (или позже: *has worked for ten years*).

Имена стека: *Vue 3, TypeScript, WebRTC, SIP* (с заглавными, *TypeScript* слитно).

### Anki / error bank

- *an app for traders* (не *a traders*)
- *But I feel I need to improve my skills*
- *They work a lot* · *has ten years of experience*
