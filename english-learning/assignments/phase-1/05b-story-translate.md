## Mentor feedback

**Вердикт:** смысл ясен, *want to / need to / don’t have* в целом на месте. Оценка: **6/10**.

### Corrected version

```text
On Saturday morning I get up late. I want a quiet breakfast in my apartment. The weather is good / nice, so I want to go for a walk. But first I need to do one thing — go to the store. I am a little / a bit hungry. I don’t have any milk or bread. My neighbor is also going outside. He is very friendly. We talk by the door for a short time. I am tired after the week, and I don’t want to work a lot on the weekend. In the evening I want to cook dinner and rest. I want to build a useful habit — to go to bed early.
```

### Главные правки (≤3 + бонусы)

1. *The weather is well* → **The weather is good/nice** (*well* ≠ «хороший» для погоды).
2. *I am some hungry* / *I taired* → **I am a bit hungry** · **I am tired** (*be* + прилагательное).
3. *don’t want a lot to work* → **don’t want to work a lot**; *get down sleep* → **go to bed early**.

Ещё: *also go* → **also goes / is also going**; *frendly/tolking* → **friendly / talking**; *before the week* → **after the week**; *a useful habit*.
