# Theme 2 — Present Continuous vs Present Simple

**Simple** = факт, привычка, «вообще так». **Continuous** = сейчас, в процессе, временно, «в эту минуту / этот период».

Не путай: *I working* — неверно. Либо *I **work***, либо *I **am working***.

---

## 1. Present Simple (повтор)

| | Формула |
|--|---------|
| Утверждение | I **work** · He **works** |
| Отрицание | I **don’t work** · He **doesn’t work** |
| Вопрос | **Do** you work? · **Does** he work? |

**Когда:** постоянная работа, расписание, факты.

```text
I work as a developer.
I work five days a week.
He develops the UI.
```

---

## 2. Present Continuous

### Формула

**am / is / are + глагол-ing**

| | Утверждение | Отрицание | Вопрос |
|--|-------------|-----------|--------|
| I | I **am working** | I **am not working** | **Am** I working? |
| he/she/it | He **is developing** | He **isn’t developing** | **Is** he developing? |
| you/we/they | They **are testing** | They **aren’t testing** | **Are** they testing? |

- *isn’t = is not*, *aren’t = are not*, разговорно *I’m not working*
- **-ing:** work → working, develop → developing, test → testing
- после *am/is/are* **нет** do/does

### Когда Continuous

| Ситуация | Пример |
|----------|--------|
| **Сейчас** (в момент речи) | I’m working on a bug **now**. |
| **Временно** (не всегда так) | I’m learning English **this month**. |
| **Процесс** (ещё не закончил) | We’re **developing** a new feature. |

Маркеры: *now, right now, at the moment, today* (иногда), *this week*.

---

## 3. Simple vs Continuous — главное

| Simple | Continuous |
|--------|------------|
| I **work** remotely. (факт) | I’m **working** remotely **today**. (сегодня так) |
| She **develops** the UI. (обычно её зона) | She’s **developing** the login page **now**. (прямо сейчас) |
| They **use** Vue. (стек) | We’re **using** Pinia **on this project**. (на этом проекте) |

**State verbs** (feel, know, need, want, like) — чаще **Simple**, не Continuous:  
✓ *I **need** help.* ✗ *I am needing help.*

---

## 4. Примеры под работу

```text
— What do you do?        — I develop web apps.           (Simple — профессия)
— What are you doing?    — I’m fixing a bug.             (Continuous — сейчас)

I work on the dealing console.                    (проект в целом)
I’m working on the contact card feature today.    (конкретная задача сейчас)

Sergey is a team lead. He leads the team.         (роль — Simple)
Sergey is leading the stand-up right now.         (сейчас — Continuous)
```

---

## 5. Частые ошибки

| Неверно | Верно |
|---------|--------|
| I working | I **work** / I **am working** |
| I am work | I **am working** |
| He is develop | He **is developing** |
| Do you working? | **Are** you working? |
| I work now on a bug | I’m **working on** a bug **now** |

## 6. Мини-диалог (запомни пару)

```text
A: Are you busy?
B: Yes, I’m working on a task.
A: What do you usually do at work?
B: I develop web applications for traders.
```

Следующая тема: Prepositions (*at work, in a bank*).
