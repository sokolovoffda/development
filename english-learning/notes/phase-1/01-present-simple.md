# Theme 1 — Present Simple (мини-конспект)

Для фактов о работе, привычках, постоянных вещах. Не для «прямо сейчас в эту секунду» (это Continuous — Theme 2).

В Present Simple у тебя **два разных набора** — не смешивай:

1. **Обычный глагол** (*work, develop, need, want*) → утверждение без помощника; ? и «не» через **do / does / don’t / doesn’t**.
2. **be** (*am / is / are*) → свои формы; **без** do/does.

---

## 1. Обычный глагол (*work*, *develop*…)

### Формула

| | Утверждение | Отрицание | Вопрос |
|--|-------------|-----------|--------|
| I / you / we / they | I **work** | I **don’t work** | **Do** you **work**? |
| he / she / it | He **works** | He **doesn’t work** | **Does** he **work**? |

- *don’t = do not*, *doesn’t = does not*
- после *do/does/don’t/doesn’t* глагол **без -s**: *Does he **work**?* (не *works*)
- в утверждении у *he/she/it* обычно **-s**: *works, develops, needs, wants*

### Примеры — утверждение (просто факт)

```text
I work as a web developer.
I develop software for traders.
I need English for work.
I want to read documentation.

He works as a developer.          ← he → -s
She develops web apps.            ← she → -s
My colleague needs English too.
```

### Примеры — отрицание (*don’t / doesn’t*)

```text
I don’t work on weekends.
I don’t need a clear goal yet.
We don’t use jQuery.

He doesn’t work in a bank.
She doesn’t develop mobile apps.
It doesn’t support IE11.          ← it → doesn’t + глагол без -s
```

### Примеры — вопрос (*Do / Does*)

```text
Do you work as a developer?
Do you need English at work?
Do they use Vue?

Does he work remotely?
Does she develop the UI?
Does your stack include Pinia?
```

Короткие ответы: *Yes, I do. / No, I don’t. / Yes, he does. / No, he doesn’t.*

## 2. Глагол *be* (*am / is / are*) — «есть / являться»

**Без** *do/does*. Неправильно: *Do I am…*, *I don’t is…*, *I be a developer*.

### Формула

| | Утверждение | Отрицание | Вопрос |
|--|-------------|-----------|--------|
| I | I **am** … | I **am not** … | **Am** I …? |
| he / she / it | He **is** … | He **isn’t** … / is not | **Is** he …? |
| you / we / they | They **are** … | They **aren’t** … / are not | **Are** you …? |

*isn’t = is not*, *aren’t = are not*. Разговорно часто *I’m not*.

### Примеры — утверждение

```text
I am a developer.
I am a web developer.
He is a trader.
She is a QA engineer.
My stack is Vue 3, TypeScript, and Pinia.
They are developers.
We are on the same team.
```

### Примеры — отрицание

```text
I am not a backend developer.
I’m not a designer.
He isn’t a manager.
She is not in the office today.    ← факт/расписание; Continuous — позже
My stack isn’t Angular.
They aren’t traders.
```

### Примеры — вопрос

```text
Am I clear?
Are you a developer?
Is he a trader?
Is your stack Vue?
Are they on your team?
```

Короткие ответы: *Yes, I am. / No, I’m not. / Yes, he is. / No, he isn’t.*

---

## Рядом: не путать два типа

| Смысл | Обычный глагол | *be* |
|-------|----------------|------|
| Кто я / что есть | — | *I **am** a developer.* |
| Что делаю по работе | *I **work** as…* / *I **develop**…* | — |
| «Не» | *I **don’t** work…* | *I **am not**…* |
| Вопрос | ***Do** you work…?* | ***Are** you…?* |

```text
✓ I am a developer. I work at Satel. I develop software.
✓ Do you work with TypeScript? — Yes, I do.
✓ Are you a frontend developer? — Yes, I am.
✗ Do you are a developer?
✗ I don’t am a developer.
✗ I be a developer. / My stack it is Vue.
```

## Частые маркеры

*every day, usually, often, sometimes, never* — часто с Present Simple.

## Чего пока не делать

| Неверно | Верно |
|---------|--------|
| I working | I **work** (или *I am working* — Theme 2) |
| i development | I **develop** |
| On work | **At** work |
| My stack it is | My stack **is** |
| Do you are…? | **Are** you…? |
| I don’t am… | I **am not**… / I’m not… |

## Мини-текст под тебя (смесь двух типов — так и надо)

```text
I am a web developer.
I work as a frontend developer.
At work I develop software for traders.
My stack is Vue 3, TypeScript, and Pinia.
I need English for work.
I want to read technical documentation.
I don’t have a clear goal yet.
I’m not a native speaker.
```

Следующая тема после закрепления: Present Continuous vs Simple (*I’m working* vs *I work*).
