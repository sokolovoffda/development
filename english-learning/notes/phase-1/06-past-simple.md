# Theme 6 — Past Simple (intro)

**Когда:** законченное действие / факт **в прошлом** (вчера, на прошлой неделе, в 2024, *ago*).

Не путай с Present Simple (*I work* = обычно) и Continuous (*I’m working* = сейчас).

Как и в Present, здесь **два набора**: обычный глагол и *be*.

---

## 0. Картина целиком

| | Present Simple | Past Simple |
|--|----------------|-------------|
| Факт сейчас / обычно | I **work** | — |
| Факт в прошлом | — | I **worked** |
| Отрицание | I **don’t** work | I **didn’t** work |
| Вопрос | **Do** you work? | **Did** you work? |
| *be* | I **am** / He **is** | I **was** / He **was** / They **were** |

---

## 1. Обычный глагол — утверждение

### Regular (правильные) → основа + **-ed**

| Present | Past |
|---------|------|
| work | work**ed** |
| develop | develop**ed** |
| want | want**ed** |
| need | need**ed** |
| talk | talk**ed** |
| cook | cook**ed** |
| walk | walk**ed** |
| play | play**ed** |
| live | liv**ed** |
| decide | decid**ed** |

Для **всех** лиц одна форма: *I/he/she/we **worked*** — **без** *-s* как в Present!

```text
Yesterday I worked from home.
Last week he developed a new feature.
On Saturday I cooked dinner.
We talked by the door.
```

### Irregular (неправильные) — запомнить

| Present | Past | Перевод |
|---------|------|---------|
| go | **went** | шёл / пошёл |
| get | **got** | получил / встал (*got up*) |
| have | **had** | имел / завтракал (*had breakfast*) |
| do | **did** | делал |
| make | **made** | сделал |
| come | **came** | пришёл |
| see | **saw** | видел |
| write | **wrote** | писал |
| read | **read** | читал *(пишется так же, звучит ред)* |
| eat | **ate** | ел |
| drink | **drank** | пил |
| sleep | **slept** | спал |
| buy | **bought** | купил |
| leave | **left** | ушёл / уехал |
| meet | **met** | встретил |
| feel | **felt** | чувствовал |
| find | **found** | нашёл |
| fix | fix**ed** | починил *(regular)* |
| build | **built** | построил / выстроил |

```text
Yesterday I got up late.
I went to the store.
I had breakfast in my apartment.
I ate pasta and drank tea.
I felt tired after the week.
I bought milk and bread.
```

---

## 2. Отрицание и вопрос — через **did**

В Past для обычных глаголов помощник один: **did** (для всех лиц).

После *did / didn’t* глагол всегда в **1-й форме** (без *-ed*, без Past):

| | Утверждение | Отрицание | Вопрос |
|--|-------------|-----------|--------|
| все лица | I **worked** | I **didn’t work** | **Did** you **work**? |
| | He **went** | He **didn’t go** | **Did** he **go**? |

```text
✓ I didn’t work on Sunday.
✓ She didn’t go for a walk.
✓ Did you cook dinner?
✓ Did he fix the bug?
✗ I didn’t worked…
✗ Did you went…?
✗ Did he worked…?
```

Короткие ответы: *Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t.*

---

## 3. *be* в прошлом — *was / were* (без did)

| Present | Past |
|---------|------|
| I am | I **was** |
| he / she / it is | he / she / it **was** |
| you / we / they are | you / we / they **were** |

| | Утверждение | Отрицание | Вопрос |
|--|-------------|-----------|--------|
| I / he / she / it | I **was** tired | I **wasn’t** tired | **Was** I…? |
| you / we / they | They **were** busy | They **weren’t** busy | **Were** you…? |

```text
Yesterday I was tired.
The weather was nice.
My neighbor was friendly.
We were at home.
I wasn’t hungry in the morning.
Were you busy last Monday?
```

✗ *Did you was…?* · *I didn’t was…* · *I be tired yesterday*

---

## 4. Маркеры времени (Past)

```text
yesterday
last night / last week / last Monday / last year
two days ago / a week ago
in 2024 / in August
when I was a child
on Saturday morning (если про конкретную прошлую субботу — из контекста)
```

```text
Yesterday I went to the store.
Last weekend I cooked dinner.
Two days ago I fixed a bug.
Last year I lived in another apartment.
```

---

## 5. Present vs Past — пары

| Present (обычно / сейчас) | Past (вчера / тогда) |
|---------------------------|----------------------|
| I work remotely | Yesterday I **worked** from home |
| I get up early | On Saturday I **got** up late |
| I have breakfast | I **had** breakfast at 10 |
| I go to the store | I **went** to the store |
| I am tired | I **was** tired |
| I want to rest | Yesterday I **wanted** to rest |
| I need to sleep | I **needed** to sleep |
| Do you work…? | **Did** you work…? |
| Are you busy? | **Were** you busy? |

---

## 6. *want to / need to* в Past

```text
I wanted to go for a walk.
I needed to buy milk.
I didn’t want to work a lot.
I didn’t need to deploy yesterday.
Did you want to cook dinner?
```

После *wanted to / needed to* глагол снова в **1-й форме**: *wanted to go* (не *wanted to went*).

---

## 7. Мини-история (образец)

```text
Last Saturday I got up late.
I had a quiet breakfast in my apartment.
The weather was nice, so I wanted to go for a walk.
But first I needed to go to the store.
I was a bit hungry.
I didn’t have any milk or bread.
My neighbor also went outside.
He was very friendly.
We talked by the door for a short time.
I was tired after the week, and I didn’t want to work a lot.
In the evening I cooked dinner and rested.
I wanted to build a useful habit — to go to bed early.
```

Сравни с твоим Present-текстом P1-05b: те же идеи, но всё **в прошлом**.
